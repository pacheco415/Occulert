FAQ

Questions drivers
always ask.

Everything you need to know before you start driving with Occulert.

Does it work if I wear glasses or sunglasses?+
Yes — Occulert works with most prescription glasses. Clear-lens glasses have no impact on detection accuracy. Tinted sunglasses may reduce accuracy slightly, but the app still functions. We recommend removing very dark sunglasses at night.
Does it work at night or in low light?+
Yes. Occulert is optimized for low-light driving conditions. The AI works under dashboard glow, streetlights, headlights from oncoming traffic, and even near-total darkness. Night driving is one of our core use cases.
How does it alert me? Will I hear it?+
Alerts fire through multiple channels simultaneously: your phone speaker at high volume, connected Bluetooth earbuds, and haptic vibration on Apple Watch. You will hear it — even with music playing or windows down.
Is my camera footage stored or uploaded anywhere?+
Never. Occulert processes all camera data on your device in real-time. No video, images, or biometric data is ever saved or transmitted anywhere. Your camera feed exists only in live memory while the app is running.
Does it drain my battery quickly?+
Occulert uses approximately 8–15% battery per hour, similar to GPS navigation. We recommend plugging your phone into your car charger while using it on long trips.
Does it work without internet?+
Yes — completely. Once loaded, Occulert runs entirely offline. It works in tunnels, dead zones, rural areas, and anywhere with no signal.
What phone mount should I use?+
Any standard dashboard or windshield mount that positions your phone's front camera at roughly face level works perfectly. Magnetic mounts, vent clips, and suction cup mounts all work great.
Is Occulert really free?+
Yes, completely free. No subscription, no account required, no hidden fees. Occulert is kept free through voluntary donations from drivers who find it valuable.
