Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work with glasses or sunglasses?+
Yes. Tested with prescription glasses and most sunglasses. During automatic calibration, the AI learns your personal eye baseline.
Does it work at night?+
Yes. Works in darkness, tunnels, and varying road lighting. Turning on the interior light during calibration improves accuracy.
How does it send alerts to my Apple Watch or earbuds?+
Occulert uses the Web Vibration API for haptics. Your Apple Watch or Galaxy Watch receives vibration through the paired phone. Earbuds receive audio alerts through the phone.
Is my video recorded or uploaded anywhere?+
Never. All processing happens 100% on your device. No video, images, or biometric data is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted.
Does it drain my battery?+
Camera and AI are power-intensive. We recommend plugging into your car charger during long drives.
How do I install it on my home screen?+
iPhone: Open occulert.com in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". Android: Open in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu, then "Install App".
Is Occulert really free?+
Yes, 100% free. No subscription, no credit card. If it helps you stay safe, consider supporting via our GoFundMe campaign.
