FAQ

Questions drivers
always ask.

Everything you need to know before you start driving with Occulert.

Does it work if I wear glasses or sunglasses?+
Yes — Occulert works with most prescription glasses. Clear-lens glasses have no impact on detection accuracy. Tinted sunglasses may reduce accuracy slightly, but the app still functions.
Does it work at night or in low light?+
Yes. Occulert is optimized for low-light driving conditions including dashboard glow, streetlights, and near-total darkness.
How does it alert me? Will I hear it?+
Alerts fire through multiple channels: your phone speaker at high volume, connected Bluetooth earbuds, and haptic vibration on Apple Watch. You will hear it — even with music playing.
Is my camera footage stored or uploaded anywhere?+
Never. Occulert processes all camera data on your device in real-time. No video, images, or biometric data is ever saved or transmitted anywhere.
Does it drain my battery quickly?+
Occulert uses approximately 8–15% battery per hour, similar to GPS navigation. Plug your phone into your car charger on long trips.
Does it work without internet?+
Yes — completely. Once loaded, Occulert runs entirely offline. It works in tunnels, dead zones, and anywhere with no signal.
Is Occulert really free?+
Yes, completely free. No subscription, no account required, no hidden fees. Kept free through voluntary donations.
