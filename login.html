Sign in to Occulert

Use the email and password for your existing Occulert account.

Already added a new Occulert passkey? Use Touch ID, Face ID, your device PIN, or a security key. Otherwise, sign in with email and password first, then choose Account Setup → Add a passkey.

or use email and password

Fleet manager? Sign in normally, then choose Manage Fleet. Fleet access is verified by server ownership—not by this form.

Passkeys are in beta. First confirm your Occulert account, then add a passkey from Account Settings. Google sign-in is coming later.
Privacy: Cloud storage is optional. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are never stored by Occulert. Passkey biometrics, PINs, and private keys stay with your device or password manager. Fleet membership still requires protected server ownership or an administrator invitation.
