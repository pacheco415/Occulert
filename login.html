Privacy and account security
Passkeys are in beta. Confirm your email once, then create a passkey. You can always request an email sign-in link if your passkey is unavailable.
Cloud storage is optional. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are never stored by Occulert. Passkey biometrics, PINs, and private keys stay with your device or password manager. Fleet membership still requires protected server ownership or an administrator invitation.
