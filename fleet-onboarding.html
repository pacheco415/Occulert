Fleet setup

Set up your fleet

Create your protected fleet and invitation links, then return to the dashboard. You choose how to deliver each link. Each invitation works once and expires after seven days.

For a managed free trial, send a trial request. Creating a fleet here does not enroll it, start the 30-day clock or activate a paid plan.

From setup to first report

  1. Account: Checking your sign-in.
  2. Fleet: Checking for a protected fleet.
  3. Drivers: Create invitations, deliver each link, then check accepted status below.
  4. First report: Open the fleet dashboard after a driver deliberately enables sharing and completes a session. Local-only sessions do not appear there.
