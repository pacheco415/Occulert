Safety Disclaimer

Last updated: June 14, 2026

Occulert is an assistive drowsiness detection tool. It cannot guarantee crash prevention and must not be used as a reason to drive while tired.

Do not drive drowsy

If you feel sleepy, impaired, distracted, or unable to focus, pull over safely and rest. No app, alert, wearable, or AI system can replace sleep or safe judgment.

Detection limits

Occulert may be affected by lighting, phone position, camera quality, sunglasses, face angle, road vibration, browser limitations, battery mode, and device performance. False positives and missed alerts are possible.

Browser limitations

The web app may pause or reduce functionality if the screen locks, the browser goes into the background, camera permission is removed, or the operating system restricts sensors. Native mobile apps may provide stronger reliability in future versions.

Fleet use

Fleet managers should treat Occulert as one safety layer, not a replacement for driver training, rest policies, legal compliance, vehicle maintenance, or professional safety programs.

Emergency guidance

If Occulert alerts, reduce speed only when safe, maintain control of the vehicle, and pull over in a legal and safe location. Do not interact with the app while actively driving.
