📱 HOW IT WORKS

Three Steps to Safer Driving

No installation needed. Occulert runs entirely in your browser — powered by on-device AI that never sends video anywhere.

1
📱
Mount Your Phone
Place your phone on your dashboard or windshield mount, front camera facing you. No special hardware needed — just your phone.
2
👁️
AI Calibrates to You
Occulert measures your personal eye openness baseline in the first few seconds. Then it tracks your Eye Aspect Ratio 30 times per second using 468 facial landmarks.
3
🚨
Drive — We Watch
The moment your eyes close longer than your threshold, Occulert fires an alert — vibration on your phone, haptics on your watch, audio through your earbuds. All in under 200ms.
100% Private: All AI processing happens on your device. No video is ever recorded, uploaded, or stored anywhere.

❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Occulert.

Yes. Occulert is designed to work with most glasses and sunglasses. For best results with dark or mirrored lenses, increase the sensitivity threshold in the app settings. The AI detects eyelid movement, not pupil color.
Occulert uses your phone's front-facing camera. Most modern iPhones and Android phones have infrared sensors that work in low light. For best night performance, keep your phone's display brightness up or use an infrared-capable mount.
Occulert triggers phone vibration, which transfers to your Apple Watch or Galaxy Watch via the Bluetooth connection. Audio alerts play through whatever audio device is connected — AirPods, Galaxy Buds, your car's Bluetooth, or your phone speaker.
Never. All AI processing happens entirely on your device using WebAssembly. No video frames, images, or personal data are ever transmitted to any server. Occulert cannot see, record, or store your camera feed.
Everyone's eyes are different. Calibration measures your personal "open eye" baseline so alerts are triggered by YOUR drowsiness patterns, not a generic average. It takes about 3–5 seconds and only needs to be done once per session.
Yes. A windshield or dashboard mount is the ideal setup. Mount your phone so the front camera has a clear view of your face. The AI works from a wide range of angles and distances.
Yes. Occulert detects your phone's orientation and adjusts automatically. Both portrait (vertical) and landscape (horizontal) modes are fully supported.
