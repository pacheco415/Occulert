How It Works

Three steps.
One life saved.

Mount your phone, open Occulert, and drive. The AI handles the rest.

Step 01
📱
Mount Your Phone
Clip your iPhone or Android to your dash mount so the front camera faces you. Works with any standard phone mount — no extra hardware needed.
Step 02
👁
AI Watches Your Eyes
Occulert tracks 468 facial landmarks and calculates your Eye Aspect Ratio 30x per second. The moment your eyes stay closed too long — it knows before you do.
Step 03
🚨
Instant Multi-Alert
Phone vibrates, watch pulses, earbuds sound a warning — all at once. You wake up before anything goes wrong. Lives saved by milliseconds.
Features

Built for real roads.
Not just demos.

Every feature engineered for real driving — day or night, glasses, any lighting.

👁
468-Point Eye Tracking
Google MediaPipe FaceMesh tracks 468 landmarks. Calculates Eye Aspect Ratio with medical-grade precision 30 times per second.
⚡
Sub-Second Alerts
Detection to alarm in under 200ms. Fast enough to prevent a microsleep from becoming a tragedy on the highway.
📳
Multi-Device Alerts
Phone vibration, smartwatch haptics, and earbud audio fire simultaneously. Impossible to sleep through.
🔒
100% On-Device Privacy
All AI runs on your phone. No video recorded, no data uploaded. Your face never leaves your device. Ever.
⚙️
Adjustable Sensitivity
Tune the detection threshold for your driving style — from a quick commute to a 12-hour overnight haul.
📊
Session Event Log
Every alert timestamped and logged. Review your drowsiness history to understand your fatigue patterns over time.

Occulert was created by Richard Pacheco — an automotive technology student and fleet driver from San Francisco who experienced the danger of drowsy driving firsthand and decided to do something about it.

🚛
Built From Real Experience
As a U-Haul transfer driver and security officer working overnight shifts, Rich saw the danger of drowsy driving every day — and knew there had to be a smarter solution.
🎓
Grounded in Automotive Knowledge
Pursuing an AS in Automotive Technology at Skyline College with a focus on Fleet Service Management — Occulert bridges real automotive knowledge with cutting-edge AI.

Free For Every Driver
Safety shouldn't have a price tag. Every driver — whether they drive a U-Haul or a school bus — deserves protection. Occulert will always be free to use.
