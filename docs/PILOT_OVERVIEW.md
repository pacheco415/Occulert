# Occulert: a small, voluntary fleet pilot

**Proposal:** one fleet owner, up to five willing drivers, 30 days, with reviews
at days 7 and 30. The introductory trial has no charge, no credit card, and no
automatic renewal. Any paid service requires a separate agreement.

Occulert is a supplemental prototype that uses a phone's front camera to
estimate eye/fatigue patterns and provide alerts. This pilot evaluates setup,
participation, alert experience, and manager workflow. It does not establish
crash prevention, detection accuracy, or fitness to drive.

## What participants can evaluate

- Foreground browser monitoring with on-device camera processing, phone alerts,
  sensitivity controls, and local session history.
- Voluntary signed-in cloud sync and a fleet-owner dashboard showing protected,
  client-reported session summaries, 7/30-day views, and saved follow-up outcomes.
- A privacy-limited report export and a read-only browser/AirPlay TV display
  showing fleet aggregates without identifying individual drivers.
- An installed private iPhone/TestFlight build, if available and specifically
  included in the agreed pilot. Unreleased native and branch features are not
  promised as part of the pilot.

A manager needs an owner account and joined drivers. Drivers need a compatible
phone, a stable legal mount, and a parked setup opportunity. Optional Watch or
headphones are not required. Test the intended alert route while parked;
connectivity or saved preferences do not prove an alert will be heard or felt.

## Privacy and consent

No camera video, images, audio, or raw motion are uploaded by these workflows.
Drivers choose whether to enable cloud sync. Local use does not automatically
populate the manager dashboard. Location is not required; keep optional GPS
off for this pilot unless separately justified and consented.

Protected manager reports exclude coordinates, personal media, and raw motion.
They contain client-reported metrics, not independently verified telemetry.
The TV display also excludes names, vehicles, individual scores, and raw events.
The latest-50-session history limit can make a 7/30-day report incomplete.

## Safe participation

Complete setup, alert tests, and review while parked; a passenger may observe.
Keep camera monitoring in the foreground. Never drive tired or deliberately
simulate drowsiness on the road. An alert, tracking failure, fatigue, or unsafe
condition is a reason to pull over safely and rest or resolve setup. Do not
restart or adjust the phone while moving.

Occulert may miss drowsiness or produce false alerts. It cannot guarantee
delivery, alertness, emergency response, or compliance, and does not replace
rest, fleet policy, attentive driving, or emergency judgment.

## Review and next step

At day 7, review consent/setup, participation, missing records, and reported
friction. At day 30, decide whether the workflow is useful, what needs repair,
and whether to stop, continue a controlled evaluation, or discuss a separate
rollout. Drivers may pause participation; agree support and local/cloud deletion
steps before starting.
