Choose Your Occulert App

Use the browser monitor now, or follow your private pilot invitation for the iPhone app and optional Watch companion. Their local histories, sign-in sessions and update delivery are separate.

Browser monitor

Open the browser driver app in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android while online. You can use it in the browser or add it to your Home Screen. Core local monitoring does not require an account.

iPhone browser setup

  1. 1

    Open Safari

    Visit occulert.com/app.html while connected.

  2. 2

    Open Share

    Use Safari's Share control, or open the page menu and choose Share, depending on your layout.

  3. 3

    Add to Home Screen

    Choose Add to Home Screen. If Open as Web App is offered, enable it, then add the icon.

Apple's Home Screen instructions

Android browser setup

  1. 1

    Open Chrome

    Visit occulert.com/app.html while connected.

  2. 2

    Open the menu

    Choose Install and create shortcut, then Install. Menu wording may differ by browser version.

  3. 3

    Follow the prompts

    Open the resulting icon and complete the parked camera setup.

Google's web app instructions

A Home Screen web app runs the website. Camera, audio, vibration and offline availability depend on the device and browser. Keep it visible with the screen awake. Visit while online and allow the offline bundle to finish before relying on cached files; cloud and map services still need connectivity.

Private iPhone and Watch pilot

The native iPhone app and optional Watch companion are distributed through private TestFlight invitations. Follow your invitation to install or update the iPhone app, then install the companion on your paired Watch if you want to try Watch alerts.

The iPhone remains the primary monitoring and alert device. Keep camera monitoring in the foreground and complete the parked setup. A Watch is optional, and a connection status does not guarantee that a notification or vibration was delivered.

A public App Store release is planned. You can express interest in the pilot; submitting interest does not guarantee an invitation. Existing participants can use Watch setup guidance.

Website updates arrive separately from TestFlight updates. Use the version and build shown in iPhone Settings inside Occulert when asking for help.

Before starting: Park safely, secure the phone, allow camera access and follow the displayed readiness steps. Do not handle the app while moving. Occulert is a supplemental prototype and may miss events or trigger false alerts.
