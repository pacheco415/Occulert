## Exact-build parked device checklist

Every row is **Not tested** on a binary built from this candidate. Use the
[device record](DEVICE_ACCEPTANCE.md) to identify the actual build, model,
iOS/watchOS versions, mount, lighting, eyewear, accessibility settings,
connection, audio route and volume. Stay parked for the entire test. Record
Pass, Fail or Not tested with the actual observation and any issue.

| Check | What to observe on the distributed binary |
| --- | --- |
| Fresh install and upgrade | Test a fresh install and an upgrade from the available prior binary. Record its build. Verify History IDs, annotations, preferences and interrupted-session recovery survive the upgrade. Preserve missing, partial and zero values. |
| Spanish and Arabic fallback | Set the iPhone's preferred language to Spanish, fully relaunch, then repeat with Arabic. Open Home, Monitor, History, Settings and Pre-drive. Check app-owned headings, warnings, consent and recovery wording remain English, with left-to-right layout even for Arabic. Check the spoken-cue language note and VoiceOver labels. Record the actual language tag for each run. |
| Watch critical haptics | Pair and enable the companion, keep the Watch app active and use a parked critical episode with fresh closed-eye status. Record the first pattern and timed wrist repeats (about 4 seconds apart). Reopen the eyes and observe the open status and cessation of the repeated pulses. Check Stop, stale status, wrist lowering/app deactivation and the 60-second maximum in separate trials. Record what the wearer felt and the time separately from receipt/acknowledgment. |
| Watch and phone fallback | Check normal alert, Watch screen asleep, Focus, disconnect/reconnect and phone fallback. Record missed, delayed or duplicate cues and any replay after reconnect. |
| Camera and accessibility | Grant, deny and restore the camera. Check tracking in intended lighting with and without eyewear. Lock, switch apps, take a call and restart while parked. Verify Stop remains reachable and monitoring status reflects actual frames with large text and VoiceOver. |
| Phone audio and safe stop | Check speaker, vibration, car and intended headphones with Silent mode on/off, low volume, Focus and navigation audio. Record what was heard/felt, delay, ducking and restoration. Verify stopping and saving locally before the Maps handoff. |
| Cloud and recovery | Try offline finish/reconnect, opt-out across restart, sign-out failure/retry and account switching. Check pending records retain their ownership and no delivery continues after consent withdrawal. |
| Optional sensors and sustained use | Deny optional Health/Motion access and exercise phone-only monitoring. Record startup, measured event-to-cue delay, stalls, temperature, battery and recovery at 30 and 60 minutes. |

Use the [full parked acceptance additions](RELEASE_2026-11-08.md#exact-build-parked-acceptance)
and [Duo checks](RELEASE_2026-10-29.md#duo-specific-acceptance) for the relevant
devices. Record Safari and the installed web app's Silent-mode checks against
their own website deployment. An Android phone is needed for the concurrent
camera probe, and a paired Android phone and Wear OS watch for the separate
wrist experiment. Neither Android check is an iPhone TestFlight result.
