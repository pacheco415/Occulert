FAQ

Questions drivers
always ask.

Everything you need to know before you start driving with Occulert.

Does it work if I wear glasses or sunglasses?+
Yes — Occulert works with most prescription glasses. Clear-lens glasses have no impact on detection accuracy. Tinted sunglasses may reduce accuracy slightly depending on how dark the lenses are, but the app still functions. We recommend removing very dark sunglasses on long drives for best results.
Does Occulert record video or store my face?+
Absolutely not. All AI processing happens entirely on your device. No video is recorded, stored, or uploaded anywhere. Your camera feed is analyzed in real-time and immediately discarded. We never see your face and never will. Your privacy is non-negotiable.
Does it work at night or in low light?+
Yes. Occulert uses your phone's front camera which performs well in low-light conditions. For best results at night, use your phone screen brightness at a medium setting. The AI is optimized to handle typical night-driving light conditions including dashboard glow and streetlights.
Will it go off every time I blink normally?+
No. Occulert is specifically designed to distinguish between normal blinks (which last about 150-400ms) and drowsy eye closures (which last much longer). The alert only fires when your eyes stay closed for an extended period across multiple frames — the pattern associated with microsleep and drowsiness, not normal blinking.
What phone mount should I use?+
Any dashboard or windshield mount that positions your phone's front camera at or near face level works great. Popular options include iOttie, Lamicall, and Spigen mounts (all around $15-30 on Amazon). The key is that your face is clearly visible to the front camera without strong backlighting from windows behind you.
Does it drain my phone battery?+
Running the camera and AI continuously does use battery, similar to using GPS navigation or video calling. We recommend keeping your phone plugged into your car charger while using Occulert on long trips. On a typical 1-hour drive with the app running, you'll use approximately 15-20% battery without charging.
Is Occulert really free? What's the catch?+
Yes, completely free. No subscription, no ads, no data selling. Occulert was built by one person — a fleet driver and automotive student — who believes safety shouldn't have a price tag. If it helps you, consider making a small donation to keep the project running. That's the only "catch."
Can I use it while using Google Maps or Waze?+
Yes. Occulert runs in your browser while navigation apps run separately. You can use split-screen on Android, or keep Occulert open and switch to Maps. The audio alerts will still fire through your earbuds even when you switch apps. We're working on a native app with true background monitoring coming soon.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

What We Collect

Occulert does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, video, or biometric information. The camera feed used for eye-tracking is processed entirely on your device in real-time and is never saved or sent anywhere. When you sign up for our waitlist or contact form, we collect only your email address and the information you voluntarily provide.

How We Use Your Data

  • Email addresses from the waitlist are used only to send product updates and fleet announcements
  • Contact form submissions are used only to respond to your inquiry
  • Google Analytics collects anonymized usage data (pages visited, device type, general location) to help us improve the app
  • We never sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes

Camera & Biometric Data

Your camera is accessed only while the Occulert app is actively running with your permission. All facial landmark analysis happens locally on your device using Google MediaPipe. No images, video, or biometric data ever leaves your phone. Occulert cannot identify who you are — it only measures eye openness ratios in real-time.

Cookies

We use Google Analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our website. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

Your Rights

You can request deletion of any data we hold about you at any time by emailing hello@occulert.com. You can unsubscribe from our email list at any time using the link in any email we send.
