Commercial and safety boundaries

Know what you are evaluating before the trial starts.

Prototype decision support

Occulert may miss fatigue events or issue false alerts. It is not a certified safety, medical, emergency, or compliance device and cannot make tired driving safe.

Privacy-limited manager reports

Protected reports exclude GPS coordinates, camera media, audio, and raw motion. Session telemetry is client-reported and not independently verified.

Managed enrollment

Submitting a request does not create a subscription or charge a card. Scope, availability, support, and final commercial terms are confirmed in writing first.

Questions

Before you request a rollout

Does the trial guarantee fewer incidents?

No. The trial helps a fleet evaluate participation, manager workflow, alerts, and client-reported observations. It does not guarantee safety outcomes or replace rest, training, policy, or professional judgment.

Does Occulert upload driver camera video?

No. Camera frames are processed on the driver's device. Protected manager reports exclude personal camera media, audio, GPS coordinates, and raw motion.

Will the free trial charge me later?

No. The trial requires no credit card and does not renew automatically. A fleet continues only after deliberately choosing and agreeing to a paid plan.

Can a larger fleet start with five drivers?

Yes. The free trial is intentionally limited to a small active group so the fleet can evaluate the workflow before choosing a broader rollout.
