Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work with glasses or sunglasses?

It is designed to work with many glasses and sunglasses, but lighting, camera angle, and lens darkness can affect accuracy.

Is my video recorded?

No. Occulert is designed for on-device processing and does not need to upload your camera feed for core detection.

Does GPS turn on automatically?

No. GPS is optional in the driver app and should only be enabled when you want location in reports or fleet demos.

How do I install it?

On iPhone, open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open in Chrome and tap Install App.
