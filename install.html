Get Started

Add to Your Home Screen

Occulert works as a Progressive Web App. Add it to your home screen for the best experience with no app store required.

Quick Install Guide

Follow these steps on your phone to install Occulert like a native app.

1

Open in Browser

Visit occulert.com in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android.

2

Tap Share or Menu

On iPhone, tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari. On Android, tap the three-dot menu in Chrome.

3

Add to Home Screen

Select "Add to Home Screen" and confirm. Occulert will appear as an app icon on your device.

4

Launch and Drive

Open from your home screen for the full-screen experience with no browser chrome.

Device Compatibility

iOS

iPhone

Safari 15 or later recommended. Keep Safari open and screen unlocked while driving. Disable Low Power Mode for best performance.

Android

Android

Chrome or Edge browser recommended. Allow camera permissions when prompted. Works on most Android 9 or later devices.

Tip: Use a dashboard phone mount with your screen facing you at eye level. Avoid direct sunlight glare on the camera for best tracking results.
