How It Works

Four Steps to Use the Prototype

Getting started takes less than a minute. No account or setup needed.

1

Mount Your Phone

Position your phone on the dashboard or windshield mount so your face is centered in the camera frame.

2

Start Monitoring

Tap the Start button and allow camera access. Occulert calibrates to your eyes in just a few seconds.

3

Keep Occulert Visible

Monitoring works only while Occulert stays open in the foreground with the screen awake. Keep your phone mounted and do not interact with the app while driving.

4

Get Alerted

If fatigue is detected, you receive an immediate alert with visual, audio, and vibration cues to prompt you to pull over and rest.

⚠️

Safety Notice: Occulert is a prototype assistant, not a replacement for adequate rest or safe driving practices. If you feel tired, pull over safely. Do not interact with the app while driving. Always follow local traffic laws.
