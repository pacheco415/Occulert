## Day-7 and day-30 review

Use a fresh authenticated report for stored session counts, participating
drivers, missing or invalid ends, and saved manager reviews. Use the invitation
list for invited and accepted counts; ask drivers about alerts and interruptions;
use the support log for requests and owner time. A report cannot measure
monitored hours or find sessions that were never recorded. Record the report
generation time and whether it explicitly confirms full database coverage for
the chosen window. If not, mark report totals **partial** and do not use them as
complete-period totals.

## Short feedback prompts

Ask participating drivers privately after parking:

1. Was setup possible without handling the phone while moving?
2. Did monitoring visibly start and stay available? Where did it stop?
3. Were any alerts useful, false, late, or apparently missed? Describe only
   a naturally occurring observation; do not reconstruct or stage a risky
   drive.
4. Was the intended sound, vibration, or Watch route actually noticed?
5. Would you choose to continue, pause, or stop? What one change matters most?

Ask the manager:

1. Did the roster, invitations, and report answer the agreed question?
2. Which records were missing or incomplete, and how did that affect review?
3. How many minutes did review and support take this week?
4. Would the manager continue under a written paid scope? Record the stated
   price/conditions and any actual commitment.
5. What must be fixed before another driver joins?

## Decision record

At day 7 choose **continue, pause, or stop**, with owner and next review date.
At day 30 choose **stop, extend a controlled evaluation, or discuss a separate
paid agreement**. Record why, unresolved issues, data deletion requests,
support burden, and the next owner action. Do not silently convert a free
trial into paid service.
