Driver and start-date filters search the whole protected fleet before loading a page. Recorded end status and sorting apply to loaded matching records; load older pages to expand those views. Account changes or leaving this page clear filters.

Recent periods include today and the preceding 6 or 29 local calendar days, through the time you apply the filter. Custom dates include each selected local day; either boundary can be left open, but enter at least one date. Future starts are excluded from date ranges. Sorting applies to loaded records; loading older sessions continues in the server’s newest-first order. Missing sort values appear last.
