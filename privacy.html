Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

Occulert is designed to process core drowsiness detection on your device. GPS and cloud fleet sync are optional and should only be enabled when you intentionally want location/session data saved for a report or fleet demo.

Camera and AI processing

Occulert uses your device camera to analyze eye openness and facial landmarks for drowsiness detection. The camera feed is intended to be processed locally in your browser or app session. Occulert does not need to upload live camera video to operate the core detection experience.

Information stored on your device

Occulert may store preferences, driver identifiers, local session reports, fatigue metrics, alert counts, and optional GPS data in your browser local storage so the app and dashboards work during testing.

Location and GPS data

GPS is optional. When you enable location access in the driver app, Occulert may record your coordinates, route sample points, speed in miles per hour, and trip distance. If cloud sync is also enabled, this data may be synced to Firebase for the fleet dashboard. You can deny GPS permission or leave the GPS setting off; core drowsiness detection works without GPS.

Cloud sync and third-party services

When cloud sync is enabled, Occulert uses Google Firebase Firestore to store session data such as driver identifiers, fatigue scores, GPS coordinates, and alert counts. This data is stored on Google's servers. Google's privacy policy applies to data stored in Firebase. Cloud sync should be used only with appropriate Firebase security rules and authentication. Occulert does not sell, share, or rent personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

Fleet inquiries

If you submit a fleet inquiry, the information you provide may include your name, company, email, fleet size, phone number, and message. That information is used to respond to your request and evaluate pilot or commercial interest.

Analytics

Occulert may use basic analytics to understand page visits, app launches, and reliability. Analytics should not include your live camera video.

Device alerts

Occulert may use vibration, sound, Bluetooth, and paired-device capabilities when supported by your device and browser. These features depend on browser, operating system, and device support.

Children

Occulert is intended for licensed drivers and fleet safety use. It is not intended for children.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to hello@occulert.com.
