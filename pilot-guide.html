Manager path

Launch a focused pilot

Open dashboard
  1. 1

    Create the protected fleet

    Sign in as the fleet owner and create one protected workspace. Keep the first pilot small so support and review stay manageable.

    Set up fleet →
  2. 2

    Invite up to five drivers

    Send each participating driver a protected invitation. Confirm they understand what the pilot collects, what it excludes, and that Occulert does not replace rest or professional judgment.

    Invite drivers →
  3. 3

    Confirm the first protected session

    Ask one driver to complete a normal session without staging fatigue. Check that the dashboard records the session before expanding participation.

    Check protected history →
  4. 4

    Use the privacy-safe shared display

    Open the aggregate-only TV view for shared visibility. It excludes driver names, vehicles, locations, individual scores, raw events, and editing controls.

    Open TV display →
  5. 5

    Review at 7 and 30 days

    At day 7, check participation, missing sessions, and support needs. At day 30, review the protected report and decide whether to continue, adjust, or stop the pilot.

Driver path

Complete a safe pilot session

Open driver app
  1. 1

    Set up only while parked

    Mount the phone securely, allow camera access, frame your face, and connect optional audio or Watch accessories before moving. Never adjust the app while driving.

  2. 2

    Start a normal session

    Keep Occulert open and visible. Monitoring stops when the app leaves the foreground. Do not stage drowsiness or drive when you should rest.

    Start parked setup →
  3. 3

    Respond to an alert by stopping safely

    An alert may be missed or may be false. If an alert occurs—or you feel tired—pull over when safe and rest. An alert never makes it safe to continue driving.

    Read safety guidance →
  4. 4

    End and save the session

    Stop the session after parking. If protected fleet sync is enabled, the manager receives the privacy-limited session summary; camera video and raw motion are not included.

    Review privacy details →
Shared expectations

What this pilot can and cannot show

Managers can review

  • Protected participation and recent sessions
  • Client-reported alerts and summary scores
  • Follow-up candidates and aggregate trends

Protected reports exclude

  • GPS coordinates and personal media
  • Raw camera and motion timelines
  • Independent proof of fatigue or fault

Occulert is decision support, not a medical device, emergency service, or sole basis for discipline, compliance, or safety decisions.
