Privacy and data boundaries
Protected fleet summaries exclude GPS coordinates by design. When signed out, this page can show opt-in GPS only from same-browser local sessions or demo data.
Protected fleet data is limited to the authenticated server-verified fleet owner. Safety scores and event telemetry are client-reported and not independently verified; do not use them as sole evidence for discipline, compliance, or emergency decisions.
