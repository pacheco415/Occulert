Camera setup and interruptions

  • Secure the phone before starting and keep your face and eyes visible to the front camera. Improve lighting or reposition the phone while parked if tracking is unavailable.
  • If camera access is denied, allow it in iPhone Settings for the native app, or in your browser and website permissions for the web app. Return to Occulert and repeat the parked setup.
  • Leaving the app, locking the phone or losing camera analysis can stop monitoring. Return while parked and follow the displayed setup or restart instructions; do not assume monitoring resumed.
  • If a browser page cannot finish loading, reconnect and reload. Avoid clearing browser storage as a first step: doing so can remove locally saved history.

The iPhone app and browser app are separate. Installing a website update does not update the iPhone or Watch app.

Phone and Watch alerts

Phone sound and vibration

While parked, review Occulert's sound and vibration preferences, the phone's volume and audio output, and any connected headphones or car audio. Browser sound and vibration availability varies by device. An enabled preference does not guarantee an alert will be heard or felt.

Optional Watch companion

Confirm the companion app is installed on the paired Watch. Open it and review the phone app's Watch setting, connection status, notification permission and Focus settings. A connection reply confirms receipt; it does not prove that a notification appeared or a vibration was felt. Tell support separately what appeared and what you felt, including whether the Watch app was open or in the background.

The iPhone is the primary alert device. Watch alerts may be delayed or unavailable. You can use core camera monitoring without a Watch, headphones or Apple Health access.

Session history and privacy controls

  • In the iPhone app, open Session History to review local summaries. Settings → Privacy & Local Data provides history and recovery-data controls.
  • Browser history belongs to the browser and device where it was saved. It is separate from native history and protected fleet records.
  • A recovered interrupted session is a partial record, not a completed drive. Missing values are not measured zeroes.
  • If saving fails, follow the displayed retry or recovery guidance. Before removing local data, review or share the summaries you intend to keep.
  • Deleting local history does not delete separately synced cloud records. Signed-in account and cloud-data controls are on the website's Account Settings page.

Fleet invitations and older sessions

Accept the invitation from your fleet owner using the intended account. If the invitation has expired or was already used, ask the owner for a new one. Drivers must choose whether to enable cloud sharing; a local session alone does not establish a synced fleet record.

Managers should sign in as the fleet owner to see protected records. The dashboard, report and shared TV view summarize the latest 50 sessions. Browse older sessions opens a separate view that loads up to 50 at a time, with a limit of 500 loaded records. Browsing older records does not expand the report's calculation window.

Protected history requires a connection and clears when the account or page changes. Refresh starts again with the newest records. Session scores and counts are unverified client reports.

Contact Occulert

Email hello@occulert.com for help, feedback, or assistance locating a data control. Opening the email link does not send a message; review the details before sending.

Useful details to include

  • Whether you used the iPhone app or website, and the app version/build or browser version.
  • Your phone model and operating-system version. If a Watch is involved, include its model and watchOS version.
  • What you expected, what happened, any displayed error text and the steps leading to it.
  • For an alert issue, whether the app was open or in the background and the audio route or Focus setting used.

No passwords, verification codes, face images, camera video, audio, precise locations, Health records or private fleet exports are needed. If you choose to include a screenshot, remove names, account details and other personal information first.

Occulert is a supplemental prototype and may produce false alerts or miss drowsiness. Support is not an emergency service.
